## What is a Computer? (什么是计算机?)
A computer is an electronic device that processes data according to a set of instructions.
计算机是一种根据一系列指令来处理数据的电子设备。
Think of it as a versatile tool that can perform calculations, store information, and help you communicate.
你可以把它看作一个多功能工具，可以执行计算、存储信息并帮助你交流。

Key components include:
主要组成部分包括：
*   **CPU (Central Processing Unit - 中央处理器):** The brain of the computer, performing most calculations.
    **CPU (Central Processing Unit - 中央处理器):** 计算机的大脑，执行大部分计算。
*   **Memory (RAM - Random Access Memory - 内存):** Temporary storage for data and programs being actively used.
    **Memory (RAM - Random Access Memory - 内存):** 正在被主动使用的数据和程序的临时存储区域。
*   **Storage (Hard Drive/SSD - 硬盘/固态硬盘):** Permanent storage for your files and software.
    **Storage (Hard Drive/SSD - 硬盘/固态硬盘):** 用于永久存储你的文件和软件。
*   **Input/Output Devices (输入/输出设备):** How you interact with the computer (e.g., keyboard, mouse, monitor).
    **Input/Output Devices (输入/输出设备):** 你与计算机交互的方式 (例如，键盘、鼠标、显示器)。

## What is Programming? (什么是编程?)
Programming is like writing a very detailed recipe for a robot; the robot (computer) will follow your instructions precisely.
编程就像是为机器人编写一份非常详细的食谱；机器人（计算机）会精确地遵循你的指令。
It's the process of giving instructions to a computer to make it perform a specific task.
它是给计算机下达指令以使其执行特定任务的过程。
These instructions are written in a **programming language** (e.g., Python), which is a special language designed for computers.
这些指令是用**编程语言** (例如 Python) 编写的，这是一种专为计算机设计的特殊语言。

## Introduction to AI, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning (人工智能、机器学习与深度学习简介)
**Artificial Intelligence (AI - 人工智能)** is a broad field of computer science focused on creating systems that can perform tasks that typically require human intelligence.
**人工智能 (AI - Artificial Intelligence)** 是一个广泛的计算机科学领域，专注于创建能够执行通常需要人类智能的任务的系统。
**Machine Learning (ML - 机器学习)** is a subset of AI where systems learn from data to improve their performance on a task without being explicitly programmed for each case.
**机器学习 (ML - Machine Learning)** 是人工智能的一个子集，系统通过从数据中学习来提高其在特定任务上的性能，而无需针对每种情况进行显式编程。
**Deep Learning (DL - 深度学习)** is a subset of ML that uses artificial neural networks with many layers (deep networks) to analyze various factors of data.
**深度学习 (DL - Deep Learning)** 是机器学习的一个子集，它使用具有许多层（深度网络）的人工神经网络来分析数据的各种因素。

In Materials Science and Biomedical research, these tools are powerful:
在材料科学和生物医学研究中，这些工具非常强大：
*   **AI/ML for Materials Discovery (AI/ML 用于材料发现):** AI algorithms can predict the properties of new, undiscovered materials by learning from databases of known materials, speeding up the discovery process. For example, predicting which new alloy might have high strength and heat resistance.
    **AI/ML 用于材料发现:** AI算法可以通过从已知材料数据库中学习来预测未被发现的新材料的特性，从而加快发现过程。例如，预测哪种新合金可能具有高强度和耐热性。
*   **AI/ML for Drug Design (AI/ML 用于药物设计):** ML models can screen vast libraries of potential drug candidates to identify those most likely to be effective against a specific disease target, or analyze medical images (like cell images) to detect patterns.
    **AI/ML 用于药物设计:** ML模型可以筛选庞大的潜在候选药物库，以识别最有可能对特定疾病靶点有效的药物，或者分析医学图像 (如细胞图像) 以检测模式。

## Introduction to Computational Simulation (计算模拟简介)
**Computational Simulation (计算模拟)** is the use of computers to imitate the behavior of a real-world system or process over time.
**计算模拟 (Computational Simulation)** 是指使用计算机来模仿真实世界系统或过程随时间变化的行为。
It allows us to perform experiments on a computer that might be too difficult, expensive, or dangerous to do in reality.
它允许我们在计算机上进行实验，而这些实验在现实中可能过于困难、昂贵或危险。

In Materials Science and Biomedical research, simulation is key:
在材料科学和生物医学研究中，模拟至关重要：
*   **Predicting Material Properties (预测材料特性):** We can simulate how atoms and molecules interact to predict the mechanical, thermal, or electronic properties of a material before it's even made. **Molecular Dynamics (MD - 分子动力学)** is a common technique where we simulate the movement of atoms and molecules over time.
    **预测材料特性:** 我们可以模拟原子和分子如何相互作用，从而在材料制造出来之前预测其机械、热学或电子特性。**分子动力学 (MD - Molecular Dynamics)** 是一种常用技术，我们通过它模拟原子和分子随时间的运动。
*   **Understanding Biological Processes (理解生物过程):** Simulations can model how proteins fold, how drugs interact with cells, or how diseases progress at a molecular level, providing insights that are hard to get from experiments alone.
    **理解生物过程:** 模拟可以模拟蛋白质如何折叠、药物如何与细胞相互作用，或疾病如何在分子水平上发展，从而提供仅通过实验难以获得的见解。

## Why Learn These Skills? (为什么要学习这些技能?)
In modern science, these skills are essential for discovery and innovation.
在现代科学中，这些技能对于发现和创新至关重要。
**Programming (编程)** is the foundation that allows you to implement AI algorithms and run simulations.
**编程**是实现AI算法和运行模拟的基础。
**AI and Machine Learning (人工智能与机器学习)** help you analyze complex data, find hidden patterns, and make predictions.
**人工智能与机器学习**帮助你分析复杂数据、发现隐藏模式并做出预测。
**Computational Simulations (计算模拟)** allow you to test hypotheses and understand systems at a level of detail often impossible through direct experimentation alone.
**计算模拟**使你能够检验假设并以通常无法通过直接实验达到的详细程度来理解系统。
Together, they form a powerful toolkit for solving complex research problems.
它们共同构成了一个解决复杂研究问题的强大工具包。
